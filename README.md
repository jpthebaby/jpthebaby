## ⚔️ Who Am I

> *"The cycle ends here. We must be better than this."* — so I mod games until they are. 😤

I'm **Gamer JP**, a game modder with **30+ games** modded, from God of War and Stellar Blade to Resident Evil and Silent Hill. I love tearing into game files, swapping characters, rewiring scripts and bringing crazy ideas to life. Every mod gets a showcase video on my YouTube channel.

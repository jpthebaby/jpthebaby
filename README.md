## ⚔️ Who Am I

> *"The cycle ends here. We must be better than this."* — so I mod games until they are. 😤

I'm **Gamer JP**, a game modder who loves tearing into game files, swapping characters, rewiring scripts and bringing crazy ideas to life in games I love. Every mod gets a showcase video on my YouTube channel.

## 🗡️ Games I Mod

| Game | Platform | What I do |
|---|---|---|
| 🪓 **God of War Ragnarök** | PC | Model / weapon swaps, Lua script mods, tweak (`.dcb`) edits |
| 🔥 **God of War III** | PS3 · RPCS3 | Character model swaps, asset extraction |
| 🥷 **Ninja Gaiden 2** | PC | Character & outfit mods |
